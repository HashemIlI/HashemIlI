## 📌 Featured Projects

### 🔍 [SupportFlow AI — Hybrid RAG for Customer Support](https://github.com/HashemIlI/SupportFlow-AI)

> A production-shaped retrieval system that knows when **not** to answer.

`Python` `Streamlit` `ChromaDB` `BGE Embeddings` `BM25` `Cross-Encoder Reranking` `Groq API`

- Built a hybrid retrieval pipeline combining **dense embeddings (BGE) with BM25**, fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion and refined with cross-encoder reranking.
- Achieved **100% Hit Rate@5** and **95.9% Mean Reciprocal Rank** on an 810-question, leakage-free held-out test set.
- Calibrated an **abstention gate** that declines unsupported questions — **90.2% answerability accuracy (F1 94.3%)**, which is the difference between a helpful assistant and a confident liar.
- Shipped a Streamlit app with conversation memory, semantic answer caching, and cited sources, backed by a **73-test automated suite**.

---

### 🎥 [Smart-City CCTV Violence Detection (SCVD)](https://github.com/HashemIlI/SCVD)

> Real-time video understanding with an evidence trail a human can audit.

`Python` `PyTorch` `OpenCV` `ResNet18 + LSTM` `YOLO11n` `Gradio` `Streamlit` `Plotly`

- Built a **CNN + LSTM video classifier** (frozen ResNet18 backbone, 2-layer LSTM) classifying footage as *Normal*, *Violence*, or *Weaponized* — **99.8% test accuracy**.
- Designed a **sliding-window inference pipeline** (2s window, 1s stride) with temporal smoothing and event merging to localise incidents on a timeline.
- Integrated a parallel **YOLO11n person-detection branch** plus an automated Arabic explainability report with downloadable HTML evidence export.
- Deployed **dual Gradio and Streamlit dashboards** sharing a single inference core, with an interactive probability chart and incident timeline.

---

### 🛒 [E-Commerce Management System](https://github.com/HashemIlI/E-Commerce_Management_System)

> The full analyst loop: clean it, model it, then say what to do about it.

`Python` `SQL` `Pandas` `Scikit-learn` `Matplotlib/Seaborn` `Power BI`

- Built an **end-to-end EDA and ML pipeline** on a real-world retail dataset — data cleaning, feature engineering, and predictive modelling.
- Applied **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) clustering** to segment customers into 3 personas, and found the **top 10 products drive over 60% of total revenue**.
- Built regression and classification models for sales forecasting and segmentation, surfacing a **Q4 seasonal revenue surge** and a link between repeat-purchase rate and discount depth.
- Delivered an **interactive Power BI dashboard** translating findings into revenue, retention, and product-strategy recommendations.
